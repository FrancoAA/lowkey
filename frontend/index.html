    General

    How the app starts and looks, and how it hears you.

    Starts in the background (no window), so the keys always work.

    Light or dark. System follows your desktop's setting.

    With "System default": a Bluetooth headset's microphone switches it to call mode, with poor sound for everything else. Another microphone is used when there is one.

    Auto-detect works for most speech. Set it when the app picks the wrong one.

      Meeting settings

      How meetings are recorded, and how their notes are written.

      Meeting key

      …

      When Zoom, Meet, Teams, Slack or another call app uses the microphone, the meeting strip offers to record it. Choose per call: ask, always record, or never.

      Screen video

      During a meeting, Record screen saves the screen, or one window, as a video with the meeting (10 frames a second, about 450 MB for one hour). A moment of the transcript plays it from there. The first time, your desktop asks what to share.

      Who is speaking

      Off

      After a meeting stops, each of the others gets a label (Speaker 1, Speaker 2…) that you can name. The first time, two models download (46 MB). While a meeting records, the others' sound is kept in a temporary file on this computer, and deleted when the voices are told apart.

      Notes

      Writes the notes of a meeting from its transcript, one section for each topic, and answers your questions about the meeting. The notes are a draft: check them against the transcript.

      On by default. The first time, the chosen built-in model downloads (once), with its progress in the meeting.

      Notes model

      … · Change in Models

      What the model is told before it reads the meeting. {language} becomes the meeting's language; the names in Vocabulary are added at the end. Change it to try other notes: Restore the original brings back the app's instructions. Then write the notes of a meeting again to see the result.

      In the note

      AI agents

      Let Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI and other agents read your meetings and dictations, and transcribe files with the speech model on this computer. They connect over MCP.

      Connect an agent

      Run the line for your agent once, in a terminal. The agent starts it when it needs it: this window does not have to be open.

      An agent sends what it reads to its own AI service. Nothing else leaves this computer.

      Off: agents only read and transcribe. On: an agent can start or stop a meeting recording when you ask it to. You see the strip and a notification each time.

      What agents can do
      • List and read meetings: the notes, your notes and the transcript ("Me" is you).
      • Search meetings and dictations.
      • Get action items from the notes, by person.
      • Read the last dictation.
      • Transcribe a file on this computer, with the speech model chosen in Speech.
      • See if a meeting is being recorded, and start or stop one when the switch above is on.
